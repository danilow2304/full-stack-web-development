Native plants are plants that naturally occur in a particular region. They provide food and shelter for local wildlife.

Peperomia

Peperomia is a genus of small, tropical plants that are native to Central and South America. They are known for their attractive foliage and are popular as houseplants.

Height 2-3 Feet.
Bloom Time Summer
Sunlight Mid Sun
Water Low to Moderate
