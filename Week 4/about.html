About Us

Welcome to the Native Plant App! Our mission is to educate and inspire people about the importance of native plants in Pennsylvania. We believe that by promoting the use of native plants, we can help preserve biodiversity, support local ecosystems, and create beautiful landscapes.

Our Goal

We aim to provide a comprehensive resource for learning about native plants in Pennsylvania and promoting their conservation.
